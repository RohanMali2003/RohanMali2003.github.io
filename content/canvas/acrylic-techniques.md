# Acrylic Technique Log

Studio notes on acrylic painting techniques.

---

## Color Mixing

Working with a limited primary palette:

- **Cadmium Yellow** — warm, opaque.
- **Cadmium Red** — warm primary.
- **Ultramarine Blue** — cool blue, granulating.
- **Burnt Sienna** — warm earth tone for neutrals.
- **Titanium White** — opaque tinting.

### Neutral Grays
Mix complementaries rather than pure black:
- Orange + Ultramarine -> warm neutral gray
- Red + Green -> earthy brown-gray

---

## Medium Ratios

| Purpose | Ratio |
|---|---|
| Thin wash | 80% water, 20% acrylic |
| Translucent glaze | Acrylic glaze medium + thin coat |
| Standard application | Tube paint straight |
| Heavy impasto | Gel medium / modeling paste |

---

## Layering

1. **Toned Ground**: Apply a thin raw sienna wash to eliminate blank canvas white.
2. **Block In**: Establish large shapes and value structure.
3. **Refine**: Sharpen edges and contrast in focal areas.
