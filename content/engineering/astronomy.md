Log of celestial observations and space science notes.

## Naked-Eye Observations

Reference for locating key constellations and stars:

- **Winter**: Orion, Sirius (magnitude -1.46), Pleiades cluster in Taurus.
- **Summer**: Scorpius, Galactic core, Jupiter.
- **Polaris**: Altitude aligns roughly with latitude.

## Core Topics

- **Stellar Evolution**: Main sequence -> red giant -> white dwarf / neutron star / black hole depending on initial solar mass.
- **Cosmic Distance Ladder**: Parallax -> Cepheid variables -> Type Ia supernovae.
